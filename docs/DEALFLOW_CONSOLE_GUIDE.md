## Quick Start Workflow

### Step 1: Choose High-Demand Region

Click a **Quick Start** button:
- **TRT in Texas** (highest demand)
- **GLP-1 in California** (rapidly growing)
- **TRT in Florida** (strong market)
- **GLP-1 in Texas** (emerging)
- **GLP-1 in Florida** (growing)
- **IV Therapy in Arizona** (niche opportunity)

Or configure custom:
- Region: "Texas", "Florida", "California", etc.
- Service Line: TRT, Weight Loss/GLP-1, IV Therapy
- Max Clinics: 20-50 (start with 20)

### Step 2: Export Dealflow Batch

Click **"Export Dealflow Batch"**

The system will:
1. Find clinics in that region
2. Enrich their profiles (services, pricing, CTAs)
3. Check if they're running ads
4. Generate outreach packs for each
5. Rank by money potential (high/medium/low)

**Time:** 2-5 minutes for 20 clinics

### Step 3: Review Ranked List

You'll see:
- **Stats Dashboard:** Total, High/Medium/Low priority, With Ads, Contacted, Responded, Closed
- **Estimated Total Value:** e.g., "$500K+ annual potential"
- **Ranked Table:** Clinics sorted by priority score

**Focus on HIGH priority clinics first.**

### Step 4: Execute Outreach

For each clinic, click **"View Pack →"** to see:
- Priority assessment & why-now
- Talking points
- **First Email** (ready to copy)
- **LinkedIn DM** (ready to copy)
- **Call Script** (ready to copy)

**Daily Targets:**
- **5-10 emails** per day
- **2-3 LinkedIn DMs** per day
- **1-2 warm calls** per day

### Step 5: Track Status

As you contact clinics, update status:
- **Email Sent** → Click "Copy & Mark Sent" on email
- **LinkedIn Sent** → Click "Copy & Mark Sent" on LinkedIn DM
- **Called** → Click "Copy & Mark Called" on call script
- **Responded** → Click "Mark Responded"
- **Meeting Scheduled** → Click "Meeting Scheduled"
- **Closed Deal** → Click "Closed Deal" 🎉

Status is saved in localStorage and persists across sessions.

## Outreach Best Practices

### Email Outreach

1. **Send 5-10 per day** (morning batch)
2. **Personalize slightly:**
   - Reference their clinic name
   - Mention their service line
   - Cite their region if relevant
3. **Track as you send:** Click "Copy & Mark Sent"
4. **Follow up after 3-5 days** if no response

### LinkedIn Outreach

1. **Send 2-3 per day** (afternoon)
2. **Connect first, then DM:**
   - Send connection request with note
   - Wait for acceptance
   - Send DM using script
3. **Target decision-makers:**
   - Clinic owners
   - Marketing directors
   - Founders

### Warm Calls

1. **Make 1-2 per day** (late afternoon)
2. **Best for:**
   - Clinics that responded to email/LinkedIn
   - High priority clinics you haven't reached
   - Follow-up calls after initial contact
3. **Use call script opener** to get attention
4. **Keep it short:** 2-3 minutes max

## Tips for Closing

1. **Lead with value, not features**
   - "We help you reduce wasted ad spend" not "We have AI routing"

2. **Use specific numbers**
   - "We've helped clinics reduce cost per lead by 30-40%"
   - "Average clinic sees 2x qualified patient volume"

3. **Make it easy to say yes**
   - Start with free audit or pilot
   - Low monthly commitment ($500-1,500)
   - Clear ROI metrics

4. **Follow up consistently**
   - Day 1: Initial outreach
   - Day 3-5: First follow-up
   - Day 7-10: Second follow-up
   - Day 14+: Final follow-up or move on

5. **Track what works**
   - Which regions convert best?
   - Which service lines respond most?
   - Which offer (audit/pilot/starter) closes fastest?

## Troubleshooting

**"No clinics found"**
- Try a different region
- Remove service line filter
- Check region spelling

**"Outreach pack seems generic"**
- Enrichment may have failed
- Try manual website review
- Use talking points as starting point

**"No responses"**
- Increase volume (10-15 emails/day)
- Try different regions
- Test different offers (free audit vs pilot)
- Personalize more

**"Status not saving"**
- Check browser localStorage
- Refresh page
- Statuses persist across sessions
